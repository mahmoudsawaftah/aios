---
name: item-space-release
description: Use when preparing an approved release or deployment. Do not deploy or publish without explicit authorization.
---
1. Read release/deployment docs, current branch/status, and required checks.
2. List changes, known risks, migrations, backup and rollback needs, and unresolved gates.
3. Verify the exact target and environment; never infer production versus staging.
4. Stop before deployment, publishing, or external changes unless specifically authorized.
5. Report release readiness from evidence; do not equate a checklist with a successful deployment.
