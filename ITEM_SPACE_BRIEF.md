# Item Space — confirmed working brief

This file records the latest known direction. It is not a finalized product specification. Codex must surface unresolved decisions before they become code.

## Direction
Build a modular business operating system with connected workflows and a clear, easy setup experience. Prioritize reliability, security, and a coherent user experience over adding modules for their own sake.

Potential business types discussed: retail and grocery, salons, restaurants, service businesses, and dental or cosmetic clinics. This list is not an approved MVP scope.

## Known product principles
- Start with one narrow, testable first release; do not attempt to build every planned module at once.
- Shared capabilities should work consistently across business types; vertical-specific workflows should be isolated where needed.
- Clinic and patient records require especially strict privacy and access control.
- Prefer evidence-based architecture and incremental changes over speculative rewrites.

## Not yet confirmed
- First customer type and MVP workflows.
- User roles, tenant model, data-retention requirements, and supported jurisdictions.

Before implementation depends on any unresolved item, present the decision and a recommendation with trade-offs. Do not silently turn earlier ideas, backlog entries, or AI-generated documents into requirements.
